        Skin Wisdom

        Good Habits & What to Avoid

        Simple principles for healthier-looking skin.

        Good Habits

        • ✓ Cleanse gently twice a day
        • ✓ Moisturise morning and night
        • ✓ Apply sunscreen every day
        • ✓ Stay well-hydrated throughout the day
        • ✓ Remove makeup before sleeping
        • ✓ Change pillowcases regularly
        • ✓ Keep your routine simple and consistent
        • ✓ Patch-test new products first
        • ✓ Eat a balanced, varied diet
        • ✓ Get adequate sleep each night

        Things to Avoid

        • ✕ Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days
        • ✕ Scrubbing skin too harshly
        • ✕ Using very hot water on your face
        • ✕ Popping or picking at spots
        • ✕ Touching your face frequently
        • ✕ Using too many new products at once
        • ✕ Sleeping with makeup on
        • ✕ Over-cleansing your skin
        • ✕ Ignoring the neck and chest area
        • ✕ Expecting overnight results
        Beauty Routine

        Build Your Routine

        Simple steps for every part of your day.

        🌅

        Morning

        Start fresh & protected

        1. 01
          Gentle Cleanser

          Remove overnight oils with a mild cleanser

        2. 02
          Toner / Mist

          Optional: balance and prep skin

        3. 03
          Eye Cream

          Gently dab around the eye area

        4. 04
          Moisturiser

          Lock in hydration for the day

        5. 05
          Sunscreen SPF 30+

          The most important step — every single day

        ☀️

        Daytime

        Maintain & refresh

        1. 01
          SPF Top-Up

          Reapply sunscreen every 2–3 hours outdoors

        2. 02
          Facial Mist

          Optional: refresh and cool your skin

        3. 03
          Lip Balm

          Keep lips moisturised throughout the day

        4. 04
          Stay Hydrated

          Drink enough water for skin from within

        5. 05
          Avoid Touching Face

          Minimise bacteria transfer

        🌙

        Night

        Repair & restore while you sleep

        1. 01
          Remove Makeup

          Use a gentle micellar water or cleansing balm

        2. 02
          Double Cleanse

          Follow with a gentle water-based cleanser

        3. 03
          Serum (Optional)

          Target specific concerns like hydration or texture

        4. 04
          Eye Cream

          Gently pat around the orbital bone

        5. 05
          Night Moisturiser

          Use a richer cream to support overnight repair
